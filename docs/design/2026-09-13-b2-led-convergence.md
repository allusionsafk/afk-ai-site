# AFK AI B2-Led Design Convergence

## Decision

Use Claude's recovered B2 Instrument System as the visual and structural thesis. Import exactly one D2 idea: an asymmetric, figure-like product-proof composition. AFK AI remains the primary product; Allusions appears only as the subordinate maker identity.

## Recovered evidence

- B2 source artifacts: `Instrument2Desktop.dc.html`, `Instrument2Mobile.dc.html`, and `Instrument2Products.dc.html` in the recovered Claude canvas workspace.
- D2 source artifacts: `Modernist2Desktop.dc.html`, `Modernist2Mobile.dc.html`, and `Modernist2Products.dc.html` in the recovered Claude canvas workspace.
- Both directions are complete visual prototypes, not production implementations. Their remote fonts, inline CSS, provisional claims, and placeholder image zones cannot ship unchanged.
- No clean AFK AI product screenshot was found. The site must use an HTML/CSS setup-path schematic and preserve a clearly labelled future demo seam.

## Product hierarchy

1. AFK AI is the page subject, headline, primary navigation context, and only download CTA.
2. Friend Beta `0.1.7rc1` is visible above the fold and at the final CTA.
3. Allusions appears as maker/studio identity in a quiet registration line and footer.
4. Adaptive Media remains a separate existing route and does not enter the AFK product narrative.

## Information architecture

1. Product masthead and compact navigation.
2. AFK AI hero with `/download`, requirements, Friend Beta status, and setup-path schematic.
3. Three explicitly separated truth bands: Shipping now, Friend Beta limits, Development direction.
4. Asymmetric product-proof figure for Control Center capabilities, derived from D2's composition but rendered with real text and semantic HTML.
5. Local/network boundary explanation.
6. Future demo seam labelled as a reserved product demo location, without simulated UI.
7. Final Friend Beta CTA and Built by Allusions footer.

## Visual system

- Preserve the self-hosted Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Mono files.
- Use B2's ruled bands, reference labels, hanging numbers, compact evidence rows, and pale technical-paper surfaces.
- Increase contrast over the prototype and provide a complete dark theme.
- Use squared or lightly rounded geometry; enclosure is evidence, not decoration.
- Keep motion subtle and disable it under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At 390 px the reading order becomes one column; at 768 px proof compositions remain legible; at 1440 px the editorial grid is fully expressed.

## Claims contract

### Shipping now

- Local model inference on the user's PC.
- Local Open WebUI account and chat history.
- User-facing services on loopback, with the documented Ollama host-bind/firewall qualification.
- Control Center visibility into service health, machine information, model guidance, and privacy-aware diagnostics.
- Internet use for setup, model downloads, updates, and optional external web search.

### Friend Beta limits

- Windows 11, about 40 GB free, hardware virtualization required, NVIDIA GPU recommended.
- Virtualization, WSL, Docker first-launch, and restart blockers may appear late.
- The installer does not promise to launch Control Center automatically.

### Development direction

- Earlier and clearer prerequisite detection.
- More context-aware hardware/model guidance.
- A real 20–60 second product demonstration when clean capture material exists.

### Rejected

- One-click, zero-setup, any-PC, permanently offline, self-healing, optimal-model, or automatic-fastest-model claims.
- Fabricated dashboards, terminal output, screenshots, customer metrics, testimonials, or benchmark results.

## Technical invariants

- Semantic static HTML, external CSS, and minimal external JavaScript.
- Strict self-only CSP with no inline script/style and no third-party subresources.
- Existing Cloudflare Worker and Worker identity remain unchanged.
- `/download` tag, URL, filename, and SHA-256 pin remain byte-for-byte unchanged.
- No deploy or merge in this pass.
